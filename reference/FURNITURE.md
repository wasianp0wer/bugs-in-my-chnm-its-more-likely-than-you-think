# RRCHNM (Research Hall, 4th floor): interior reference and furniture inventory

The photos were collected 2026-11-09. Most come from **RRCHNM20** (https://20.rrchnm.org), the center's 20th-anniversary Omeka archive. Item pages only load with a `.html` suffix, e.g. `https://20.rrchnm.org/items/show/365.html`. Five more photos come from Dan Cohen's blog post "The New Center for History and New Media" (dancohen.org/?p=89, July 17, 2006). Those images were recovered through the Wayback Machine.

**Room-number caveat:** none of the sources give GMU room numbers (461/462/470/472/478/479). The labels "main room", "computer lab" and "west wing" below come from captions; matching them to room numbers is inference. Here is what the captions do establish:
- In July 2006 CHNM took about half of the top (4th) floor of "Research I", now Research Hall, as three unconnected office suites (Cohen 2006; item 365).
- In June 2009 CHNM took over the corner space of the former Autonomous Robotics Lab, known as the **"Robot Room"**. It is a large open area that became the "big room" with "a bank of windows" (items 365, 451). **This is most likely the "main room" you are modelling.**
- A **computer lab** existed from 2006 (Cohen photo). The "Lower 48" metal map was hung in it in 2008 (item 371), and the 2013 staff photo was taken in front of that map (item 428).
- The **"Rosenzweig Forum"** is a meeting room with a hanging banner; it appears in photos from 2013.

## 3. Finishes and windows

- **Ceiling (seen 01, 06, 07, 11, 15):** suspended acoustic tile ceiling in white, mostly 2x2 tiles, with 2x4 recessed fluorescent troffers (prismatic lenses) in regular rows. Estimated height 9 ft. Exit signs and fire strobes are visible.
- **Walls (seen):** painted gypsum board, white to warm off-white. Square columns are drywall-clad (01). Window piers between bays are white drywall (02).
- **Floor (seen):** broadloom carpet. It is tan/gold/beige with a fine texture in the CHNM suites (01, 02, 04, 05). The common areas and classrooms have grey or patterned grey carpet tile (fallback photos 22–25).
- **Doors (seen 09, 12, 16, 07):** light oak/maple veneer with a tall narrow vision lite, lever handles and hollow-metal frames painted beige.
- **Windows:**
  - Main/big room: a "bank of windows" along the exterior wall (item 451 caption). Photo 02 shows large rectangular windows, about 5 ft wide and 5–6 ft tall, sill about 2.5 ft above the floor. They have white aluminum mullions and are grouped in pairs between white piers about 2 ft wide, with white horizontal mini-blinds.
  - Offices and the lab: separate punched windows, about 3 ft wide by 5 ft tall, with a horizontal mullion at about 2/3 height so they look like double-hung windows (04, 13, 14, 17).
  - Exterior photos 28 and 29 match this. The brick wings have punched windows in pairs; the metal-panel wings have continuous ribbon windows. The robot-room corner is probably on a metal-panel/ribbon-window section, which would explain the wider window bank. This is inferred.
- **Lighting extras (seen):** an owl table lamp in the lounge and a torchiere in the meeting room.

## Gaps

- No photo is tied to a room number. The main-room identification rests on captions: the "Robot Room" (2009) and the "big room" with a bank of windows (2009, 2013).
- There is no wide shot of the main room in daily use after 2009. Desk layout, rows and counts there are only partly seen.
- No photos of the pantry/kitchenette, fridge or kitchen counter were found.
- Nothing more recent than 2014 was found; the furniture may have changed since then.
- Flickr, Twitter/X, Instagram and GMU News searches turned up no further interior shots. The full-size Flickr originals behind several RRCHNM20 images (e.g. Flickr user "orpost" for THATCamp 2012) were not explored further.
